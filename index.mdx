Welcome to the katbose.dev documentation. This site covers a personal portfolio platform that doubles as a project showcase, technical blog, Things I Explore (TIE) notes, recruiter resume portal, and AI-searchable representation of work. The entire system is built as a documentation-led monorepo: the 20-part specification in `docs/` and `PLAN.md` are the authoritative source of truth, and the implementation follows from them.
